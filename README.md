# Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics Internship
**Task 1: Business Sales Performance Analytics**

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing business sales data to identify sales trends, evaluate profitability, and understand product and regional performance using Microsoft Power BI.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Understand sales distribution across categories.
- Compare sales performance across regions.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Dataset
**Dataset:** Sample Superstore Dataset

The dataset contains information about orders, products, categories, regions, sales, quantity, discounts, and profit.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- CSV Dataset

## Data Preparation
- Imported the dataset into Power BI.
- Checked data quality and missing values.
- Verified column data types.
- Prepared the data for visualization and analysis.

## Dashboard Features
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Total Orders
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Profit by Category

## Key Insights
- Technology recorded the highest sales among the product categories.
- The West region generated the highest sales.
- Technology contributed the highest profit.
- Furniture showed comparatively lower profitability.
- The dashboard helps identify business performance patterns.
